Pro tips

Brewing basics

Coffee-to-water ratio

Start with a 1:16 ratio for most brews. For example, 20 g coffee to 320 g water creates a balanced cup.

Ideal water temperature

Keep water between 92°C and 96°C. Lower temperatures emphasize clarity; higher settings increase extraction and body.

Grind size guide

Use coarse for French press, medium for V60 and Chemex, and fine for espresso and moka pot setups.
